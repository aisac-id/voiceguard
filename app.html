How to use VoiceGuard

Scan a meeting

  1. Join the meeting in a normal Chrome tab.
  2. Here pick Source → Meeting / tab audio, press Share tab / screen….
  3. Choose the meeting tab and tick “Also share tab audio” — without that checkbox there is nothing to analyze.
  4. Watch the verdict: green REAL, amber SUSPECT, red SYNTHETIC.

The five signals

  • Pitch life — intonation spread & micro-jitter of the voice fundamental.
  • Breath floor — real rooms carry breath and hiss under pauses; TTS renders digital silence.
  • Spectral churn — organic articulation changes are uneven; vocoders are metronomic.
  • Spectral balance — formant TTS swings violently between buzz and noise bands.
  • Voice imperfection — breathy turbulence between harmonics; synthetic vowels are too clean.

Honest limits

This is heuristic triage, not forensic proof. High-quality neural voice clones can pass; heavily processed or noise-gated real voices can dip into SUSPECT. Treat red as “look closer”, never as an accusation. Use with consent.
